# 🏰❤️ Critter Battle Tower 3D: Heart Mob Climb!

🚧 **In development.** It will be live here when released: [https://kunruiw1991.github.io/critters-battletower3d/](https://kunruiw1991.github.io/critters-battletower3d/)

A kid-safe, Minecraft-style voxel 3D web game (Three.js r161) for a 5-year-old player:

- ❤️ Throw hearts at grumpy mobs to turn them into friends. Each friend wears a floating pixel heart and joins your parade.
- 🏰 Climb a mossy-cobblestone Battle Tower: Zombie Hall, Spider Den, Skeleton Gallery, Creeper Garden, Breeze Loft, Big Swarm, and an Iron Golem on the roof.
- 🧒 Zero text, zero voice-over, at most 6 big buttons, hold-to-walk and tap-to-throw controls, and gentle respawns.

Inspired by the Bilibili Minecraft Battle Tower episode *随心配?(10) 战斗高塔* (BV1hkpt6oEfU, by Jt-sun) and reimagined as a heart-friendship game. Fan-made and not affiliated with Mojang or Microsoft.
